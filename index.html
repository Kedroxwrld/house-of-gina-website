Quick answers

Before you ask

Orders placed before 2pm are dispatched the same day. Kampala deliveries arrive within 24–48 hours, while upcountry and international orders take 3–7 business days. You’ll get a tracking link by email as soon as your parcel leaves the studio.

We offer easy 30-day returns on unworn items with tags attached. Start a return from your account or message us on WhatsApp and we’ll arrange a pickup or drop-off. Refunds are processed within 5 business days of inspection.

Absolutely. Every product page has a size guide, and our stylists can compare measurements for you. Send your usual size and a photo reference on WhatsApp and we’ll recommend the best fit — free of charge.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, mobile money (MTN MoMo and Airtel Money), and cash on delivery within Kampala. All card payments are processed through an encrypted, PCI-compliant checkout.
